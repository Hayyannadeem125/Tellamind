## 1. Brand foundation

### Positioning

TellaMind ek Michigan-based digital studio/software house hai jo businesses ke liye websites, apps, AI agents, automations aur digital growth services provide karta hai. Brand ko practical, thoughtful, modern aur approachable feel hona chahiye. Sirf “AI company” ya sirf “social media agency” ke taur par position na karein.

### Recommended tagline

> **Intelligence that moves your business forward.**

### Supporting message

> We build websites, apps, AI agents, and digital systems that help ambitious businesses do their best work.

### Brand voice

- Clear, confident, useful aur human.
- Technical terms ko simple language mein samjhayein.
- “Guaranteed growth”, guaranteed Snap Star approval, invented statistics, fake reviews ya client claims use na karein.
- Client projects aur TellaMind ke demo concepts ko visibly alag rakhein.

### Target audiences

- Startups aur founders
- Small businesses, local shops aur new businesses
- Creators aur public figures
- Real estate businesses
- Clinics aur service providers
- Worldwide businesses jo remotely kaam karna chahte hain

### Primary calls to action

1. **Start a conversation** / **Request a project estimate**
2. **Book a meeting** (real booking URL milne ke baad)
3. Secondary: **Explore our concepts**

## 6. Visual design system

### Design references

- [AI Cloud Data Platform SaaS UI](https://dribbble.com/shots/27792444-AI-Cloud-Data-Platform-SaaS-Web-UI-UX): AI/cloud visual language, layered gradient surfaces, product UI feel.
- [Software Company Landing Page](https://dribbble.com/shots/25337194-Software-Company-Landing-Page): software company content hierarchy and landing-page structure.

References se inspiration lein; exact layouts, artwork, illustrations ya copy reproduce na karein.

### Look and feel

- Light/off-white base, calm SaaS UI, generous whitespace.
- Deep navy/ink text; periwinkle/indigo/violet accent gradients.
- Soft borders, restrained shadows, rounded cards, fine grid/orbit motifs.
- Headings Manrope (or similar geometric sans); body DM Sans (or system sans fallback).
- Buttons readable and high-contrast. Motion subtle; prefers-reduced-motion respect karein.
- Abstract AI/cloud hero artwork custom SVG/CSS/approved asset ho; mobile par decorative movement/lightweight version.

### Starting color tokens

- Page background: `#F5F7FB`
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`
- Primary text: `#182337`
- Muted text: `#758094`
- Border: `#E4E9F1`
- Indigo accent: `#616DF1`
- Violet accent: `#A28AF7`
- Dark CTA: `#1C2940`

Final contrast accessibility check ke baad tune karein.

### Responsive behavior

- Wide desktop: max content width approx. 1160–1280px; three-column service/project grids.
- Tablet: two-column cards; navigation and artwork fit smaller viewport.
- Mobile: single-column hero/work/contact; service cards two columns when readable, otherwise one column; compact header; no horizontal overflow.
- Breakpoints should be chosen based on layout, not specific phone models.

## 8. SEO, performance, accessibility, and privacy

### SEO

- Har page ka unique title, meta description, canonical URL aur single descriptive H1.
- Human-readable routes and internal links.
- Open Graph/social preview image and favicon.
- XML sitemap, robots file, semantic headings, meaningful image alt text.
- Local business details sirf approved address/phone/service-area ke mutabiq; private home address publish na karein.
- Structured data sirf real business facts ke liye; fake ratings/reviews nahin.

### Performance

- Images optimized (WebP/AVIF where supported), lazy-load below-fold artwork.
- Fonts limited weights and preferably self-hosted/consent-reviewed.
- Avoid heavy 3D libraries in MVP; static/animated CSS/SVG artwork first. Heavy 3D later, with mobile fallback and reduced-motion support.
- Keep third-party scripts limited and document what each one collects.

### Accessibility

- Keyboard navigation and visible focus states.
- Form labels, helpful error messages, correct button names.
- Text/background contrast, scalable type, reduced-motion support.
- Images with informative alt text; decorative images marked decorative.
- Do not rely on color alone to convey meaning.

### Privacy and trust

- Publish a business-specific Privacy Policy before collecting production form submissions or enabling analytics/ad pixels.
- Publish Terms/Service terms where appropriate; owner/legal reviewer to approve.
- Explain what contact data is collected and why; collect only necessary fields.
- Never request Snapchat password, banking credentials, or sensitive medical data via website form.
- Add cookie consent only if site’s actual analytics/advertising setup requires it; configure by relevant markets after advice.
